الرحمن

Ar-Rahman

The Most Gracious / The Beneficent

The One who wills goodness and mercy for all His creatures. His Mercy is all-encompassing, touching every soul, every plant, and every atom in the vast universe without distinction.

menu_book

Root Words

Root ر ح م

The root R-H-M (ر-ح-م) refers to mercy, compassion, and tenderness. It is also the same root word for 'womb' (Rahim), signifying the encompassing protection and nourishment a mother provides her child.

Spiritual Meaning & Benefits

Spiritual Focus

Contemplating Ar-Rahman helps the heart find tranquility by recognizing that Divine Mercy precedes everything else. It encourages the believer to be merciful toward others.

Reflective Action
  • ✦ Practice forgiving one person who wronged you today.
  • ✦ Recite "Ya Rahman" 100 times after Fajr for inner peace.
