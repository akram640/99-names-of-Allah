ق

Qaf

Derived from the Arabic root Kh-L-Q, which signifies the act of measuring out or determining the proper measure of something. It refers to the one who brings things into existence from non-existence, perfectly proportioned and defined.

  • To create without any pre-existing model.
  • To proportion and measure perfectly in accordance with wisdom.
